# Adversarial review — Aether growth engine

**Review date:** 13 August 2026 (AEST)  
**Overall verdict:** **NOT PRODUCTION-READY.** The system has no demonstrated compliance enforcement, no demonstrated deduplication or suppression state, no verifiable feedback loop, and no path from its current assets to daily paid conversions. It is a small Gmail/Google Workspace workflow with draft content, not an autonomous, production-safe growth engine.

## Scope and evidence standard

I refreshed and read the live Google Sheet (all five requested tabs), both live Google Docs, and the live pricing and signup pages on 13 August 2026. The named Sheet and Docs are real and accessible. Direct attempts to obtain the **current** scheduler object through the required `pplx-tool schedule_cron {"action":"list"}` command were rejected with `tool_not_allowed`; the scheduler tool was also rejected at schema lookup. Therefore, no claim that depends solely on the current cron text is credited as implemented. A missing or inaccessible control is a production-audit failure, not evidence that the control exists.

The Australian Communications and Media Authority (ACMA) says commercial email requires consent, accurate sender identification and contact details, and a functional unsubscribe facility; the sender bears the responsibility to prove consent, and opt-outs must be honoured within five working days. [ACMA guidance](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam)

## Prioritized production blockers

1. **Stop claiming the job is running until execution is evidenced.** Three days after kickoff, the Sheet still has only manual kickoff entries, despite the claimed six-per-day schedule. Treat the task as failed or unauditable until current task text and run history are available.
2. **Disable all autonomous commercial sends immediately** until the system has a real consent register, suppression list, concrete compliant footer, unsubscribe parsing, and send-time enforcement. The current placeholder is not compliance.
3. **Expose and version the actual scheduled-task definition.** A system that cannot be inspected cannot be audited, debugged, or trusted. Include prompt text, enabled connectors, expression, timezone, background/exact settings, and run history.
4. **Implement idempotent inbound handling.** Thread/message-level locking and sent-state recording are mandatory before six recurring runs per day can safely reply to email.
5. **Build the revenue path before claiming revenue automation.** Publish price, add paid CTAs, make checkout work, and instrument the entire funnel. The current page cannot convert a willing visitor into a payer.
6. **Replace cosmetic learning with measurable experimentation.** Require a run log and traceable decision/outcome linkage; otherwise the “self-learning” claim is marketing, not system behavior.
7. **Keep LinkedIn draft-only by technical design.** Enforce a connector/tool allowlist and a runtime test rather than relying on prose.
8. **Remove or substantiate marketing assertions.** The current calendar should not present unverified market statistics, performance claims, or purchasable plan details as settled fact.
